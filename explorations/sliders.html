tune-panel: sliders, round 2

Six ways to change a number, all real: drag them. Each has the same two values (Perspective 200 to 2400 px, Damping 4 to 60). Everywhere: Shift for fine steps, Alt for coarse, arrow keys once focused. The grey tick or line marks the default. Under each one, "Grip" says what you hold. Then, further down, four answers to A/B.

Comparing versions (instead of A/B as it is now)

The A/B switch was unclear because B starts as a silent copy of A and nothing says so. These are the patterns other tools use. Lightroom's backslash key shows the photo before your edits while you hold it. Audio plugins (FabFilter, Waves) pair an A/B switch with an explicit "Copy A to B" button. Design and 3D tools save named snapshots. Or there's the cheapest option: no compare at all. Each mock below tilts the square by "Tilt" so you can see the difference.
